Definition

Any material used to fill the spaces of your building to reduce heat flow by reflection and/or absorption.

Insulation types include:

  • Fiberglass
  • Foam
  • Mineral wool

Supply Chain Overview

Material Composition:

Mineral wool:

  • 70% slag
  • 11% Feldspar
  • 12% Trap Rock
  • 4% Resin
  • 3% Other

Fiberglass:

  • 30% Sand
  • 30% Cullet
  • 15% Soda Ash
  • 15% Borates
  • 10% Feldspar

Foamular XPS:

  • 59.4% Polystyrene
  • 24.8% Polystyrene (Recycled)
  • 14% Blowing Agent
  • 1% Additives

Average recycled Content:

  • Mineral wool: 65%
  • Fiberglass: 75%
  • Foamular XPS: 20%
